# As a user
Here are the general stakeholder requirements regardless of user
Must have:
- SR-M-01: I want to add/edit/delete courses
- SR-M-02: I want to add/edit/delete lectures and assignments in the courses
- SR-M-03: I want to add/edit/delete checkbox columns for each assignment and lecture table
- SR-M-04: I want to label new checkbox columns
- SR-M-05: I want to be able to check and uncheck the checkboxes in the checkbox column
- SR-M-06: I want to tag each course to group relevant courses together (for example semesters or domains, no different behaviours, just flat tags)
- SR-M-07: I want to have a dashboard per course to have statistics for each checkbox column counting checked and not checked entries
- SR-M-08: I want to have a dashboard per course to list the lectures and assignments that have at least one unchecked checkbox
- SR-M-09: I want to register, log in and log out, so that my courses are only accessible to me and never visible to other users

Should have:
- SR-S-01: I want to have columns not only with checkboxes but also with custom range fields with number inputs
- SR-S-02: I want to restrict the dashboard to certain courses or specific tags
- SR-S-03: I want to have a dashboard statistics for range field columns to choose between average and sum statistics (maybe average grade per assignment or sum of points to measure if bonus threshold is reached)

Could have:
Calendar feature is thought to plan the studying, prepare for exams and have overview of when is what done, not to enforce specific time constraints and deadlines
- SR-C-01: I want to have a calendar view to assign lectures to specific days (not a lecture timetable, just visual planning aid)
- SR-C-02: I want to have a calendar view to assign assignment to specific days (not to enforce a deadline, just visual planning aid)

Won't have:
- The system will not let me add deadlines to lectures or assignments
- The system will not let me collaborate with other users or share courses
- The system will not let me upload artifacts to relevant courses/lectures/assignments
- The system will not be available as a phone app in an app store
- The system will not have a spaced repetition feature to learn the material
- The system will not let me upload and manage university events, including fairs, exams, or other event dates
